How to Solve It

Step 1
Start by downloading the PDF file of the Halloween word search. For the best experience, print it on standard US Letter or A4 paper using your home or office printer. Make sure your printer settings are set to 100% scale or actual size to avoid cutting off any part of the puzzle grid. Using a good quality paper will help the print come out crisp and the puzzle easier to read. Avoid printing in 'fit to page' mode, as this can distort the layout and make the words harder to find.
⏱ Time: 5 minutes
✓ Doneness: The puzzle grid is fully visible and crisp, with no cut-off letters or blurry lines.
Step 2
When solving, start by scanning each row horizontally for the listed words, then move on to vertical columns, and finally check the diagonals. This method helps you systematically cover the entire grid without missing any hidden words. Skipping this approach might mean you overlook words hiding diagonally or backwards, which are included to add a bit of challenge. Take your time and mark off each found word on the list to keep track.
⏱ Time: 10-15 minutes
✓ Doneness: All 20 words are circled in the grid and checked off the word list.
Step 3
Use a pencil or highlighter to mark found words so you can easily erase or adjust if needed. This is helpful if you want to reuse the puzzle or share it with friends. Avoid using permanent markers directly on the printout unless you’re sure you won’t need it again. Highlighting also makes it easier to spot patterns and remaining words.
⏱ Time: Throughout solving
✓ Doneness: Words are clearly marked without damaging the puzzle sheet.
Step 4
If you’re printing the large print version, choose a thicker paper or cardstock to enhance durability. This version is great for seniors or anyone who prefers bigger letters. Double-check your printer settings to ensure the large print file prints at full size to maintain readability. This version is perfect for group activities, especially where multiple people might solve together.
⏱ Time: 5 minutes
✓ Doneness: Large print puzzle is easy to read and the grid fits well on the page.
Step 5
After completing the puzzle, check your answers using the answer key page included. This helps confirm you found all the words and can be a fun way to spot any you missed. If you’re using this in a group, consider timing each other for a friendly competition or sharing your favorite paranormal words discovered in the puzzle.
⏱ Time: 5 minutes
✓ Doneness: All words matched correctly with the answer key.

Tips for Easy Solving

Print the puzzle on matte paper to reduce glare and make it easier to read under different lighting conditions.
Matte paper prevents reflections that can make letters hard to see, especially in rooms with bright or uneven lighting. Using glossy paper might look nice but often causes glare, which slows down solving and strains the eyes. Matte paper ensures a better experience, especially for longer solving sessions.
Use a pencil with an eraser or a light highlighter when marking words to keep the puzzle reusable or shareable.
Marking words lightly allows you to correct mistakes without damaging the puzzle. This is helpful if you want to print one copy for multiple people or reuse the puzzle in different settings. Heavy ink or permanent markers make it impossible to reuse and can be frustrating if you mark wrong words by accident.
Start by searching for the longest words first, as they’re easier to spot and help build momentum early on.
Longer words stand out more and can be found quicker, giving you confidence and narrowing down the remaining search area. If you start with shorter words, you might waste time scanning small letter groups and get frustrated. This approach makes the solving process smoother and more enjoyable.
Laminate your printed puzzle to protect it and use dry-erase markers for multiple uses or group activities.
Laminating the puzzle keeps it clean and durable, especially if you plan to use it in classrooms or parties. Dry-erase markers can be wiped off easily, allowing many people to enjoy the same puzzle without reprinting. This saves time and paper, making it an eco-friendly option.
If solving with others, set a timer for a friendly competition to see who can find the most words in a set time.
Adding a time challenge brings excitement and encourages focus. It’s a fun way to engage groups and makes the puzzle more interactive. Friendly competition can motivate solvers to pay closer attention and discover words they might otherwise miss.

Who This Puzzle Is Perfect For

Printing the puzzle using 'fit to page' or scaling options instead of 100% actual size.

Why it happens Many printers default to fitting content to page, which seems convenient but distorts the puzzle grid and word placement. This causes letters to shift or get cut off, making words harder to find and ruining the puzzle’s layout.

Fix Always select 'actual size' or 100% scaling in your print settings. Double-check the print preview to ensure the entire puzzle grid fits correctly on the page without distortion. This keeps the puzzle clear and fully legible.

Using a pen or marker without eraser when solving, which can’t be corrected if you make a mistake.

Why it happens People often grab whatever writing tool is handy, not realizing that mistakes happen and can be frustrating if permanent.

Fix Use a pencil or light highlighter so you can erase or adjust marks. This makes the solving process more forgiving and enjoyable.

Ignoring diagonal and backward words, which are included to increase difficulty.

Why it happens Solvers new to word searches may only look horizontally and vertically, missing words that are placed diagonally or backwards.

Fix Be sure to scan the grid in all directions. Start with horizontal and vertical, then carefully check diagonals forward and backward to find all hidden words.

Not printing the large print version when needed, causing eye strain for some solvers.

Why it happens People might not realize there’s a large print option or assume the standard print is fine for everyone.

Fix If you or your group members prefer bigger text, choose the large print file and print it on quality paper for easier reading and a better experience.

Printing & Saving Tips

Room Temperature

Store printed puzzles in a binder or activity folder for easy access during Halloween season or anytime you want a spooky challenge. Keep them flat and dry to avoid wrinkles.

Refrigerator

Laminate your puzzles and use dry-erase markers to solve and reuse multiple times. Wipe clean after each use to keep them fresh for group activities or family game nights.

Freezer

Organize your digital PDF files by theme and season in clearly labeled folders on your computer or cloud storage. This makes it easy to find and print puzzles for future Halloween events.

Reheating

When reprinting, always use the original PDF file and set printer to 100% scale. Use good quality paper and check print preview to ensure consistent quality across copies.

Make Ahead

Print multiple copies ahead of Halloween parties or church youth group meetings. Laminating some copies allows you to reuse them year after year, saving time and resources.
